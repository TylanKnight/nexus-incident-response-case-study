## Claim boundaries

The source supports written design and analysis. It does not support claims that a real company was protected, a breach was investigated, controls were deployed, specific tools were used or service metrics improved. Assignment grades are omitted because recovered feedback lacks an unambiguous course label.

The adaptation was prepared with AI assistance for organization and editing. Its new templates and synthetic lab materials must remain distinguishable from the original academic work.
